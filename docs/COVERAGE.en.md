# School coverage

This catalog was checked against the MOE's 2025–26 institution list and student-count dataset, with Central Police University, National Defense University and National Defense Medical University added separately. Last checked: 23 November 2026.

- 122 usable presets: 46 national/military/police institutions and 76 private institutions.
- A preset only contains differences supported by official evidence and expressible by the current schema. When the university only publishes deposit instructions or delegates formatting to departments, the preset supplies the school name and is marked `name-only` or `department-defined`.
- Official documents or reproducible labelled HTML snapshots are stored here. `sources.lock.json` records their source URLs and download hashes.

## Not applicable

- National Open University: the MOE 2025–26 dataset reports no master's or doctoral students.
- Minth University of Science and Technology: the MOE 2025–26 dataset reports no master's or doctoral students; a 2026 university announcement describes graduate progression through another university.

Both institutions remain open, but currently have no applicable thesis format, so no preset is provided.

## Closed institutions

Institutions on the MOE's closed-school list are outside the collection scope. A school is not treated as closed merely because it has stopped admissions or is scheduled to close later; an operating institution with graduate students and official material remains included.
